Delete your account

Two ways to permanently delete your Electro World account and the data tied to it — from inside the app, or by request if you no longer have it installed.

Applies to Electro World Android app Package com.aistudio.electroworld.zpqxvd

01Delete from the app

If Electro World is still installed, this is the fastest way — it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Electro World app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then confirm.

02Request deletion without the app

Uninstalled the app, or lost access to your phone number? Email us and we'll delete your account for you.

03What gets deleted
