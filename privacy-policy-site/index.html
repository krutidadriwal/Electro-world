01Information we collect

The app is used to browse products, request installations, file complaints, and track invoices with Electro World. We only collect what's needed to fulfil those requests.

CategoryWhat we collectWhen
Account details Name and phone number When you register or sign in
Service requests Delivery/service address, an optional alternate contact number, and a description of the issue or item When you file a complaint or request an installation or demo
Invoices & files Invoice files you link to a request or complaint When you attach an invoice to a request
Wishlist Product categories you save When you save items to your wishlist
Device token A Firebase push-notification token tied to your device Automatically, when notifications are enabled

We do not collect precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone data — the app does not request those permissions.

02How we use it

  • To create and maintain your account, and recognise you when you sign in.
  • To process and update you on complaints, installation requests, and demo requests.
  • To locate your invoices and connect them to a request when you ask us to.
  • To keep your wishlist in sync across sessions.
  • To send you push notifications about offers, order updates, and account activity.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

03Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers that run the app on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and stores invoice files, acting as our data processor.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to your device.

Electro World staff can access your request details to respond to and resolve them. We may also disclose information if required by law.

04Push notifications

The app requests notification permission so we can send you updates about your requests, invoices, and offers. Your device receives a Firebase token used only to target notifications to your device — you can turn notifications off at any time in your device's system settings, which stops new notifications from reaching you.

05Retention & deletion

We keep your account and request history for as long as your account is active, so you can track past complaints, installations, and invoices.

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the app, or via our account deletion page if you no longer have the app installed.

This permanently removes your account along with your complaints, wishlist, and installation requests, and cannot be undone.

06Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored with our infrastructure provider, Supabase. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure.

07Your choices

  • Access & correction — update your name and phone number from the Profile screen at any time.
  • Deletion — delete your account and associated data from Profile → Delete Account, or by contacting us below.
  • Notifications — disable push notifications in your device settings.

08Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the effective date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
